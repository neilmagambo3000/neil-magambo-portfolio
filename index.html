How I work with AI

I use AI every day. AI drafts my account research and first-touch messages; I check the facts, edit, and approve every message before it goes out. I also build software with AI-assisted development: I design the architecture, review and test the code, and debug it.

The outreach side runs as a five-step workflow I built:

  1. Account brief One page on the company, with source links and a list of anything unverified.
  2. Fit check Eight written criteria, each scored with a reason and a source.
  3. Drafts A first message plus day-5 and day-12 follow-ups, each under about 90 words.
  4. Reply triage Interested, not now, wrong person, unsubscribe or unclear, each with a next step.
  5. Weekly review What worked, what stalled and what to do next.
