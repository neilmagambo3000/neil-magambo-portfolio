Purpose of this Portfolio

The purpose of my project is to build a self promotional portfolio website that presents who I am, the skills I have developed, and the projects I have completed. The problem it addresses is that a résumé alone does not always show what I can actually do. Employers want to see real work, and my portfolio makes it easy for them to explore my projects and get a clear sense of my abilities.

Who this site is for

This site is for employers and recruiters who need to quickly understand my experience, professors and mentors who want to see my progress, and for myself as a way to own my story and track how my skills grow over time.
